# Landing-page direction

Living architectural blueprint: a dark green school plan with warm white typography, pale lime crowd dots, and restrained amber accents. Canvas draws precise geometry and illustrative agents; no external scene assets or simulation engine are required.

- Background: #10251e; foreground: #f1f0df; secondary: #b9c7b8; accent: #d8ef9d.
- Display: Manrope, weight 500, tracking -0.04em. Body: DM Sans. Both use sans-serif fallbacks if Google Fonts is unavailable.
- Desktop: headline at left, large school plan at right; scroll fades headline and enlarges plan toward its corridor.
- Mobile: headline above plan; compact navigation. Reduced-motion preference disables camera movement and continuous crowd animation.
- Clear demo labelling and honest destination feedback until a real application route exists.

This is an initial prototype. Browser screenshots and visual review have not yet been completed.

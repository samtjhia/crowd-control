# Crowd Control

HackTrent 2026 browser crowd-simulation project. Users describe venues and scenarios, preview interventions, and explore crowd movement. The simulation is intended to support deterministic replay and editing past interventions. Results are planning estimates, not certified safety analysis.

## Landing prototype

Audience: hackathon judges and visitors. Help them understand the product and explore a visual demonstration quickly. Approved concept: one school, normal evacuation, blocked exit, improved plan. The prototype covers the opening, scroll-driven zoom, and a blocked-east-exit beat with a scripted queue at the west exit. Visitors can toggle the exit or follow the reversible scroll state. Rewind/replay and the application connection are future work. Illustrative motion must be labelled as a demo.
